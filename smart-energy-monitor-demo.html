Simulated demo based on "Smart Energy and Current Monitoring with Anomaly Detection Using IoT." Readings are synthetic. The detector flags a reading when it is more than three standard deviations from the rolling baseline for the current hour, and only raises an alert once that happens on two consecutive readings — this is what filters out a harmless single-reading blip (e.g. a compressor starting) while still catching a real, sustained fault.
